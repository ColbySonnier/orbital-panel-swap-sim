# Model assets and licenses

All shipped models are **built by script** from **CC0 textures**. No third-party mesh is used, so **no attribution is required**.

## TL;DR
- **Get the textures:** `python -m models.fetch_assets` (about 19 MB, skips files you already have).
- **Build the models:** `python -m models.build_models` writes OBJ + MTL + textures to `models/obj/`.
- **Every texture is CC0 1.0** from ambientCG.
- **NASA meshes were checked and not used.** See "Evaluated, not used" below for why.

## Evaluated, not used

Colby approved these and they were downloaded to `models/downloads/`. **Neither ships in a model.**

| Asset | License | Why not used |
|---|---|---|
| **NASA Shuttle RMS (Canadarm)** | NASA, free and without copyright (public domain) | **One fused mesh**, about 1080 faces, not split into links. Splitting it isn't worth it. The arm links are **rebuilt in Canadarm style** instead: white MLI booms, metal joint housings, a snare end effector. Used as a visual reference only. |
| **NASA Landsat 8** | NASA, free and without copyright (public domain) | **No textures**, only flat colors. The 4.9 x 2.7 x 2.7 m body would need a **distorting squash** to fit the 1.2 x 1.0 x 1.0 m servicer box. Its instruments and aft thrusters would **clash with the rack** on the top and bottom faces. The servicer is built from foil and metal textures instead. |

Source pages:
- Canadarm: https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/tree/master/3D%20Models/Space%20Shuttle%20Remote%20Manipulator%20System%20(Canadarm)
- Landsat 8: https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/tree/master/3D%20Models/Landsat%208

## Gantry models

Built by script from the same CC0 textures. **No new assets.**

| Model | Frame origin | Made of |
|---|---|---|
| **Rails** (in `client`) | A frame, y = ±rail_y, z = rail_z | Aluminium extrusion, steel running strips, carbon side truss up to the keel |
| **`bridge`** | Rail line at the beam center | Carbon box truss, guide rails, drive trucks with wheels on the rail |
| **`carriage`** | Plate center | T-slot plate saddling the beam, bay guides, side frames, motors, cable chain |
| **`fork_W_head`, `fork_F_head`** | Fork tip (grapple point) | Grapple heads; F adds the crossbar and arm fittings |
| **`fork_*_stageN`** | Stage's head-side end | Telescoping stages, 4 per fork (carbon W, aluminium F) |

**Assembled check:** `models/obj/previews/gantry_assembled*.png` show the whole gantry mid-pull.

## Gotchas

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `FileNotFoundError ... run fetch_assets` | Textures not downloaded | `python -m models.fetch_assets` |
| Model loads untextured in Vizard | MTL can't find its textures | Keep the OBJ, MTL and texture files **together in `models/obj/`** |
